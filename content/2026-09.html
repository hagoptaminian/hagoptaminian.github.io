Palace Walk 📕
Naguib Mahfouz
Published in Arabic in 1956, the story of a bourgeois Egyptian family living in Cairo between 1917 and 1919. Al-Sayyid Ahmad Abd al-Jawad is a successful 45-year-old merchant and father of five. He rules his household with absolute authority, sensitive above all to traditional codes of honor and propriety. His wife Amina and daughters Khadija and Aisha are prohibited from leaving the house without him, and his three sons live in fear of his authority. Outside the house, however, al-Sayyid Ahmad is a different man: he enjoys singing and drinking, late nights, and sexual affairs, and his interior life often reveals a sensitive man of romantic sensibilities. As his children come of age, and Egypt is swept by demands for independence from Britain, his carefully ordered world begins to fracture.
