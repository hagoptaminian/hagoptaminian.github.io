Palace Walk 📕
Naguib Mahfouz
The first installment in a trilogy, Palace Walk is the story of a bourgeois Egyptian family in the early decades of the twentieth century. The novel’s center of gravity is Al-Sayyid Ahmad, a middle-aged father of five and well-to-do merchant. Although he upholds a tyrannical domestic order—his wife Amina and daughters Khadija and Aisha are prohibited from leaving the house without him—outside the home he enjoys drinking and singing, late nights, and sexual affairs. In the background, WWI is ending and Egyptian resistance against British rule is gathering momentum; against his father’s wishes, Fahmy, Al-Sayyid Ahmad’s second son, becomes embroiled in the resistance.
